Program Objectives

The goals and aims that guide the MIT program's design and delivery.

Program Goals

Program Objectives

  • 1

    Provide graduate-level education in Information Technology emphasizing the acquisition of advanced concepts, tools, and technologies necessary for professional practice.

  • 2

    Prepare graduates for the industrial practice of systems integration, systems administration, systems planning, and systems implementation, maintaining the integrity and proper functionality of complex systems.

  • 3

    Develop students' capacity to conduct both technical and statistical research, contributing original knowledge to the IT discipline and addressing emerging challenges in the field.

  • 4

    Cultivate expertise in specialized IT domains including System Development, Information Management, Interactive Systems, Intelligent Systems, and Emerging Trends in Computing — each student developing deep competence in chosen areas.

  • 5

    Train students in technology and project management principles and methodologies, covering the full software development life cycle, process models, risk assessment, scheduling, budget estimation, and team leadership.

  • 6

    Foster innovation through the Capstone Project, requiring students to propose, develop, and present a computing solution addressing a real organizational or societal problem — with work presented in a national or international public forum.

  • 7

    Produce graduates who can effectively analyze user requirements and needs, designing and implementing information systems that are practical, scalable, and aligned with current industry standards and best practices.

  • 8

    Comply with and exceed the minimum standards set by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) for graduate IT programs in the Philippines, ensuring graduates are globally competitive and ethically grounded.

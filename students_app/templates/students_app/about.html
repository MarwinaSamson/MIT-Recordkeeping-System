Program Overview

About the MIT Program

The Master in Information Technology (MIT) at Western Mindanao State University emphasizes the acquisition of advanced concepts and technologies, preparing and enabling graduates for the industrial practice of systems integration, systems administration, systems planning, systems implementation, and other activities that maintain the integrity and proper functionality of a system and its components.

Graduate Competencies

Program Outcomes

01
Advanced Systems Knowledge
Demonstrate advanced understanding of operating systems, networking, database systems, and systems design principles applicable to complex IT environments.
02
Research Proficiency
Conduct rigorous technical and statistical research, including proper methodology selection, data collection, analysis, interpretation, and academic writing of research proposals and reports.
03
Systems Integration & Administration
Apply advanced skills in systems integration, administration, planning, and implementation to maintain the integrity and proper functionality of IT systems and their components.
04
Specialization in IT Domains
Demonstrate competency in at least one IT specialization — System Development, Information Management, Interactive Systems, Intelligent Systems, or Emerging Computing Technologies.
05
Project Management Leadership
Manage software development life cycles and technology projects using proven methodologies, including risk assessment, scheduling, budgeting, and team leadership.
06
Capstone Innovation
Propose, develop, and present an innovative capstone project that introduces a new application, technology, or process addressing a real organizational or societal problem — presented in a national or international public forum.
07
Emerging Technologies Literacy
Apply knowledge of modern algorithms based on Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence to solve complex, real-world problems and provide meaningful solutions.
08
Information Management Expertise
Design and implement well-planned IT-based systems for acquisition, organization, interpretation, and visualization of data that support medium to large-scale business transactions.

Program Objectives

The goals and aims that guide the Master in Information Technology program's design and delivery.

Program Goals

Program Objectives

  • 1
    Provide graduate-level education in Information Technology that emphasizes the acquisition of advanced concepts, tools, and technologies necessary for professional practice in the field.
  • 2
    Prepare graduates for the industrial practice of systems integration, systems administration, systems planning, and systems implementation, equipping them with the knowledge to maintain the integrity and proper functionality of complex systems and their components.
  • 3
    Develop students' capacity to conduct both technical and statistical research, enabling them to contribute original knowledge to the information technology discipline and address emerging challenges in the field.
  • 4
    Cultivate expertise in specialized IT domains including System Development, Information Management, Interactive Systems, Intelligent Systems, and Emerging Trends in Computing — with each student developing deep competence in chosen areas of specialization.
  • 5
    Train students in technology and project management principles and methodologies, covering the full software development life cycle, process models, risk assessment, scheduling, budget estimation, and team leadership and motivation.
  • 6
    Foster innovation through the Capstone Project, requiring students to propose, develop, and present a computing solution — introducing a new application, technology, or process that addresses a real organizational or societal problem — and to present their work in a national or international public forum.
  • 7
    Produce graduates who can effectively analyze user requirements and needs, and design and implement information systems that are practical, scalable, and aligned with current industry standards and best practices.
  • 8
    Comply with and exceed the minimum standards set by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) for graduate IT programs in the Philippines, ensuring that graduates are globally competitive and ethically grounded professionals.

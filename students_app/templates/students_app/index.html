How to Apply

Admission
Requirements

Prepare the following documents carefully. Incomplete submissions will not be processed by the Graduate School.

  • 1

    Official Transcript of Records (TOR)

    Certified true copy from your institution

  • 2

    Completed Application Form

    Available in the Downloadables menu above

  • 3

    Valid Government-Issued ID

    Photocopy and original for verification

  • 4

    Two Letters of Recommendation

    From academic or professional references

  • 5

    Statement of Purpose

    500–800 words outlining your academic goals
